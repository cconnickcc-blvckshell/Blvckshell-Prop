Executive Summary

Investment Thesis & Financial Overview

A margin-governed, contract-disciplined Ontario facilities operator targeting the underserved friction layer between cleaning vendors and licensed trades.

Summary for Lenders and Reviewers

What we do. Blvckshell is a facilities execution and accountability operator for multi-residential and light commercial properties in Ontario. We operate in the friction layer between cleaning vendors and licensed trades: recurring cleaning (common areas, garbage rooms, stairwells, washrooms), unit turnovers, turnover readiness audits, light maintenance (non-licensed, non-permit), and facilities support.

Revenue is contract-based; pilot programs reduce client friction and convert to recurring contracts. We do not sell technology; we run a service business with an internal audit and workflow system for quality and documentation.

Geographic Focus

Launch market: Windsor, Ontario. Expansion: Opportunity-driven into other Ontario cities (100,000-400,000 population). No speculative geography; new cities only when contract and capacity justify. Ontario-only; no national expansion in the planning horizon.

Financial Profile

  • Year 1 target: 2-4 sites; monthly run rate by Month 12 of $8,000-$18,000
  • Full-year revenue: $35,000-$95,000 (back-weighted ramp)
  • Margin target: 25% gross (acceptable 20-30%; floor 15% under stress)
  • Add-ons and maintenance: Minimum 30% margin
  • Founder salary: $0 in Year 1

Risk Controls

Strict contract terms and scope; tier-level margin gates; light maintenance explicitly non-licensed and non-permit; WSIB, HST, and insurance from day one; working capital and LOC sized for 30-45 day AR and slow ramp. Detailed failure scenarios and mitigations in Section 08.

Capital Ask

A modest line of credit ($15,000-$25,000) for working capital only - AR bridge and startup supplies/payouts. Not growth financing. Amount and terms detailed in Section 11.

Operations Model

Subcontractor-Heavy Delivery, Capacity & QA Structure

Scalable operations through vetted subcontractors with retained client relationships, pricing control, and margin enforcement.

Subcontractor Model

Initial delivery is subcontractor-heavy. Blvckshell retains client relationship, contract, pricing, QA, and billing; subcontractors are paid per agreed rate (payout ceiling) so gross margin is preserved.

Selection criteria: Vetted for reliability, compliance (WSIB, insurance), and willingness to follow scope and documentation. Written agreement covers scope, rate, payment terms, insurance/WSIB, confidentiality.

Payout Ceiling

Maximum labour cost per site or per visit set so gross margin stays within 20-30% (target 25%) for cleaning; ≥30% for maintenance.

Classification Safeguards

Subcontractors may have other clients; Blvckshell defines scope and outcomes but avoids employment-like control. All agreements in writing per CRA and Ontario employment standards.

Definitions, Formulas & Regulatory Summary

A. Key Financial Definitions

Revenue Contract value earned in the period (accrual basis)
COGS Direct cost: labour, supplies, other direct site costs
Gross profit Revenue minus COGS
Gross margin Gross profit divided by revenue (expressed as %)
Overhead Fixed operating expenses not allocated to a single site
EBITDA Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, amortization
Working capital Short-term assets minus short-term liabilities

B. Margin Formulas

Gross Margin = (Revenue − COGS) / Revenue

At target 25%, COGS = 0.75 × Revenue

Payout Ceiling = Revenue × (1 − Target Margin) − Supplies − Other Direct
