# Working Capital and Line of Credit Request

## Blvckshell — Ontario Multi-Residential Service Operator

---

## Purpose of Request

A line of credit (LOC) is requested to **smooth cash flow** against:

- Receivables timing (30–45 day payment terms).
- Slow revenue ramp (minimal cash in during Months 1–3).
- Seasonal or one-time timing gaps (e.g. insurance, professional fees).

The LOC is **not** for growth capital, equipment financing, or speculative expansion. Use is limited to working capital: payroll (or subcontractor payouts), supplies, and overhead when cash receipts lag.

---

## Working Capital Need (Conservative)

| Driver | Estimate |
|--------|----------|
| AR timing | 30–45 days of revenue. At $8,400/month run rate (3 sites), 45 days ≈ $12,600 outstanding. |
| Ramp period | Months 1–4: cash out (overhead + early COGS) exceeds cash in; cumulative shortfall can reach $5,000–$15,000 depending on exact ramp. |
| Buffer | One-time or timing items (e.g. insurance annual premium, year-end professional); $2,000–$5,000. |

**Total working capital need (illustrative):** $15,000–$25,000. The company plans to request a LOC of **$15,000–$25,000** primarily to bridge accounts receivable cycles and manage upfront supplies and subcontractor payouts during early contract ramp. Exact amount and terms are for discussion with the bank.

---

## LOC Structure (Proposed)

| Term | Proposed |
|------|----------|
| Facility type | Revolving line of credit. |
| Limit | $15,000–$25,000 (to be confirmed with lender). |
| Use | Working capital only: payables, subcontractor payouts, overhead, and other operating expenses when cash in is insufficient. |
| Repayment | Draws repaid from operating cash flow as receivables are collected. No fixed amortization; revolving. |
| Security | As required by lender (e.g. general security agreement; no request for real property unless lender policy requires). |
| Covenant / reporting | Monthly or quarterly reporting as required; no speculative use. |

---

## Security and Guarantee (Clarification)

The following are acknowledged for lender discussion; final terms are subject to negotiation:

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Corporate facility** | LOC in company name; repayment from operating cash flow. No personal guarantee assumed in this plan, but many lenders require it for facilities under $50k to small corporations. |
| **Personal guarantee** | If required by lender, founder may provide personal guarantee; extent (full vs. limited) to be agreed with bank. |
| **Secured by receivables** | LOC may be secured by accounts receivable (e.g. assignment or lockbox); AR aging and concentration would be disclosed. |
| **General Security Agreement (GSA)** | Lender may take a GSA over company assets; typical for small business LOC. No real property is proposed as collateral unless lender policy requires. |

The company is prepared to discuss which of these the bank requires. Acknowledging these options demonstrates realism and reduces perceived execution risk.

---

## Use of Proceeds (Examples)

- **Month 2:** Overhead $700; no revenue; draw $700 to cover.
- **Month 4:** COGS $2,100 + overhead $700; revenue $2,800 not yet collected; draw to cover gap until receipt.
- **Month 6:** Insurance premium $3,000 due; draw to pay, repay from next month receipts.

Proceeds are **not** used for: founder salary in Year 1, marketing spend beyond minimal, new geography without a contract, or capital equipment beyond normal operations.

---

## Repayment Capacity

- Operating cash flow (Section 10) shows positive net P&L from Month 4 onward in base case; cash in lags but accumulates.
- Draws are repaid when receivables are collected and cash exceeds immediate payables. In base case, Year 1 ends with cumulative net P&L positive (~$5,250); with AR delay, cash balance may be lower but sufficient to reduce or clear LOC draws by end of Year 1 or early Year 2, assuming no material adverse change.
- If revenue underperforms (low case), repayment may extend; LOC provides runway until revenue and collections improve.

---

## Summary

The company requests a modest revolving line of credit of **$15,000–$25,000** for working capital only. Use is limited to covering timing gaps between payables and receivables and to supporting the conservative revenue ramp. Repayment is from operating cash flow. This supports bank-ready discipline and reduces liquidity risk in the first 12 months.
