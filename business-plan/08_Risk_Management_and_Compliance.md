# Risk Management and Compliance

## Blvckshell — Facilities Execution and Accountability Operator

---

## Risk Disclosure Philosophy

This section identifies material risks and mitigation. It does not eliminate risk. Conservative planning and margin discipline reduce but do not remove exposure. Lenders and investors should conduct their own due diligence.

---

## Explicit Failure Scenarios and Corrective Actions

Banks appreciate contingency thinking. The following are explicit triggers and responses:

| Scenario | Trigger | Corrective action |
|----------|---------|-------------------|
| Slow ramp | Fewer than 2 sites by Month 6 | Intensify outreach in Windsor; no new geography. Revisit pricing only within margin guardrails (no sub-20% contracts). Extend runway by maintaining zero founder salary and minimal overhead. |
| Site loss | One or two sites non-renew or exit in Year 1 | Replace via pipeline; retention and reclean discipline to limit further loss. If replacement lags, draw LOC as needed; do not add fixed cost until revenue recovers. |
| Margin compression | Gross margin falls below 20% on a site or in aggregate | Identify cause (labour, supplies, or price). Reprice at renewal or re-scope; exit site if margin cannot be restored within one renewal cycle. |
| Cash shortfall | Cash out exceeds cash in for 2+ months | Draw LOC per approved use. If LOC insufficient, defer non-critical overhead (e.g. marketing); do not defer WSIB, insurance, or payroll. Seek additional working capital only with revised forecast. |
| Failure to secure LOC | Bank declines or offers terms that are not workable | Operate on cash from operations only; slow ramp further (e.g. 1–2 sites max until cash flow positive). No speculative expansion. |

## Insurance and WSIB Status (Clarification)

| Item | Status / assumption |
|------|----------------------|
| **WSIB** | To be registered and active before first worker or subcontractor performs work. No work starts without coverage in place. If subcontractors use their own WSIB, evidence of coverage is required and verified. |
| **CGL and other insurance** | Policies in place before first contract start. Certificates available for clients on request. Renewals tracked; no gap in coverage. |
| **HST** | Registered before first taxable supply. All contracts state tax treatment (inclusive or extra). |

*If any of the above are pending at plan date, state explicitly: "WSIB/insurance/HST will be in place by [date] or before first revenue."* This removes ambiguity for the bank.

## Maintenance and Facilities Risk Controls

Light maintenance (Tier 4) is offered only under strict scope and risk boundaries. The following controls protect the model legally and operationally.

### Explicit Exclusions (No Exceptions)

| Exclusion | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Licensed trades** | No electrical, plumbing, HVAC, gas, or any work requiring provincial trade qualification. Blvckshell does not hold trade licenses and does not perform licensed work. |
| **Permit-required work** | No work requiring building permits, electrical permits, or other regulatory permits. |
| **Structural modifications** | No load-bearing, framing, or structural changes. No alteration of building structure. |
| **Like-for-like only** | Replacements (e.g. bulbs, hardware) are like-for-like only. No specification changes that could affect code or warranty. |

### Scope Documentation and Evidence

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Scope document** | Every maintenance job has a written scope (task list, areas, limits). No open-ended "handyman" work. |
| **Evidence logging** | Completion evidence (photos, checklist) is captured and stored in the internal portal/system. Supports QA and dispute resolution. |
| **Trade-required flags** | When inspection or audit identifies work that requires a licensed trade, it is flagged and excluded from Blvckshell scope; client is directed to appropriate trade. |

### Insurance and Subcontractor Requirements

| Item | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Blvckshell insurance** | CGL and other coverages explicitly extend to light maintenance as defined in scope. Limits and exclusions are reviewed with broker; no coverage assumed for excluded work (licensed, permit, structural). |
| **Subcontractor insurance** | Any subcontractor performing maintenance must provide proof of CGL and WSIB (or equivalent) and agree in writing to the same scope exclusions. Certificates and scope acknowledgment are retained. |
| **Incident reporting** | Any incident (injury, damage, near-miss) during maintenance is reported and documented per the incident process; insurer notified when required. |

This section does not eliminate risk but makes boundaries clear for lenders, clients, and operators. Work outside these boundaries is declined or referred to licensed trades.

## Revenue and Client Risks

| Risk | Description | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|------------|
| Concentration | Early revenue depends on few sites; loss of one contract has material impact. | Contract terms and scope clarity to reduce dispute-driven exits; retention focus; diversification as site count grows. |
| Non-renewal | Client may not renew at term end or may re-tender. | Performance, documentation, and relationship; conservative churn assumption in forecasts. |
| Payment default | Client delays or fails to pay. | Credit check where practical; clear terms and follow-up; working capital and LOC for AR timing. |
| Scope creep | Client expects more than contract without paying. | Written scope and change-order process; no informal expansion without price adjustment. |

---

## Operational Risks

| Risk | Description | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|------------|
| Subcontractor failure | No-show, quality failure, or compliance breach. | Vetting, agreement, backup capacity where possible; reclean and incident process. |
| Labour availability | Unable to staff sites at required quality or cost. | Payout ceiling and capacity math; avoid over-commitment; consider employee mix as scale grows. |
| Quality failure | Repeated recleans or client dissatisfaction. | QA structure, checklist, evidence, and reclean policy; corrective action on patterns. |
| Incident (injury, damage) | On-site injury or property damage. | Incident process; WSIB and insurance; documentation and insurer notification. |

---

## Margin and Financial Risks

| Risk | Description | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|------------|
| Margin compression | Labour or supply cost increase, or price pressure. | Pricing engine and payout ceiling; target 25%, floor 15%; add-ons at 30%+. |
| Reclean cost | Excessive recleans erode margin. | Reclean policy and tracking; root cause and cap. |
| Cash flow | AR delay or slow ramp causes liquidity stress. | Working capital planning; LOC (Section 13); no founder salary in Year 1. |
| Overhead creep | Fixed cost grows faster than revenue. | Low fixed overhead; hiring and expansion only when revenue justifies. |

## Regulatory and Compliance

| Requirement | Risk if not met | Mitigation |
|-------------|------------------|------------|
| WSIB | Fines, orders, uninsured injury liability. | Coverage and reporting from day one; subcontractors compliant or covered as required. |
| HST | Interest, penalties, audit. | Collect and remit; clear contract language; books in order. |
| Insurance | Client requirement; liability exposure. | CGL and other required coverages; certificates provided; renewals tracked. |
| Contract law | Unenforceable terms, dispute loss. | Ontario-law contracts; scope and terms in writing; professional review where appropriate. |

---

## Strategic and Execution Risks

| Risk | Description | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|------------|
| Slow sales | Fewer contracts than planned; revenue ramp delayed. | Conservative forecast; no reliance on aggressive close rate; capacity not added ahead of work. |
| Geographic overreach | Entering a second city before capacity or pipeline supports it. | Contract-first expansion; density before geography. |
| Key person | Founder or key operator unavailable. | Documentation and systems; no single-point dependency assumed in long-term plan. |
