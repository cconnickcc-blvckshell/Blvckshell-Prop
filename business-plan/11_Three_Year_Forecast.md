## Revenue Mix and Blended Margin (Multi-Year)

Revenue categories (recurring cleaning, turnover, maintenance, facilities support, pilot) are tracked across all three years. Base case assumes:

- **Year 1:** Cleaning-dominated; small share of pilot and optional maintenance. Blended margin 25%.
- **Year 2–3:** As maintenance and recurring mix grow, maintenance may reach 10–25% of revenue; blended margin can improve to ~26–28% (see Section 12 sensitivity). Pilot conversion feeds recurring and turnover; pilot revenue share remains modest (pipeline tool).

No hockey-stick mix shift; improvement is gradual and margin-governed.

---

## Scenario Framework

Three cases are used: **low**, **base**, and **high**. They differ by site count and revenue; margin and overhead assumptions are consistent unless noted. All cases assume density-first (one city in Year 1; second city only when justified) and no founder salary in Year 1.

---

## Ontario Expansion Trigger

- **Second city:** Entered only when (a) at least one signed contract or firm commitment in that city, and (b) existing city (e.g. Windsor) is stable at 4+ sites or revenue supports the added complexity.
- **Third and fourth cities:** Same logic; revenue and capacity must support management and travel/coordination.

---

## Revenue and Overhead Scaling (Narrative)

- **Revenue:** Grows with site count and monthly contract value; some price increase in renewals possible but not assumed at scale.
- **Overhead:** Grows with insurance, professional, systems, and eventually staff. Not linear; step increases when new city or new role is added.
- **EBITDA:** Improves as revenue grows faster than overhead in base and high cases; low case remains tight.

## Five-Year Blended Model (Maintenance Ramp)

The following extends the base case to Years 4 and 5 with an explicit **maintenance ramp**: maintenance revenue grows as a share of total revenue, improving blended gross margin. Revenue categories are tracked; no hockey-stick assumptions.

### Revenue Mix Assumptions (Years 4–5, Base Case)

| Revenue category | Year 3 share | Year 4 share | Year 5 share | Note |
|------------------|--------------|--------------|-------------|------|
| Recurring cleaning | 70% | 65% | 60% | Core; share declines as other tiers grow. |
| Turnover cleaning | 10% | 12% | 12% | Stable or slight growth with site count. |
| Maintenance | 12% | 18% | 22% | Ramp as capacity and client uptake allow. |
| Facilities support | 5% | 4% | 4% | Modest; retainer where sold. |
| Pilot revenue | 3% | 1% | 2% | Pipeline; small share of total as recurring base grows. |

### Blended Margin (Years 4–5)

| Year | Blended gross margin | Driver |
|------|----------------------|--------|
| 3 | 25% | Cleaning-dominated. |
| 4 | ~26.5% | Maintenance ~18% of revenue at 32% margin. |
| 5 | ~27.5% | Maintenance ~22% of revenue at 32% margin. |

Formula (illustrative Y5): (0.60 × 25%) + (0.12 × 27%) + (0.22 × 32%) + (0.04 × 25%) + (0.02 × 35%) ≈ **27.5%**.

Maintenance ramp is capacity- and sales-led; the 22% maintenance share by Y5 is a target, not a guarantee. All figures are illustrative and depend on execution.

## Summary

Three-year forecast uses low, base, and high scenarios with conservative site counts and density-first expansion. The five-year extension adds a blended model with maintenance ramp and improved blended margin. EBITDA ranges from negative (low case Y2–Y3) to modestly positive (base and high). No hockey stick; growth is stepped and Ontario-focused.
