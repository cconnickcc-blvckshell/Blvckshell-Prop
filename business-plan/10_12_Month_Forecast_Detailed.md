## Revenue Categories (Forecast View)

Revenue is reported in five categories; Year 1 base case remains cleaning-heavy with optional pilot and maintenance ramp:

| Category | Year 1 assumption (base) | Note |
|----------|--------------------------|------|
| Recurring cleaning | Majority of revenue | Core sites; flat monthly. |
| Turnover cleaning | 0–15% of revenue | As vacancies and contracts allow. |
| Maintenance | 0–10% of revenue | Ramp as capacity and scope allow; margin ≥ 30%. |
| Facilities support | 0–5% | Retainer or per-event where sold. |

Blended margin in Year 1 base case is **25%** (cleaning-dominated). As maintenance and pilot share increase, blended margin can move toward 26–28% per Section 09 and 12.

## Ramp Model and Site Assumptions

- **Months 1–3:** 0–1 site; first contract may close in Month 2 or 3. Revenue may be zero or minimal in Month 1–2.
- **Months 4–6:** 1–2 sites; first full months of revenue from 1–2 contracts.
- **Months 7–12:** 2–4 sites; conservative ramp to 3 sites by Month 12 in base case.
- **Monthly contract value per site:** $2,800 (blended assumption; some sites higher, some lower).
- **Gross margin:** 25% (revenue − COGS) / revenue in base case; improves with maintenance/pilot mix per Section 09.
- **COGS:** Labour (75% of revenue at 25% margin), supplies (5%), other direct (e.g. 2%); total COGS 82% of revenue in this structure (alternatively: labour 70%, supplies 5%, other 0% → 75% COGS → 25% margin). For simplicity, **COGS = 75% of revenue** (labour + supplies + direct).

## Revenue Ramp Model (Narrative)

- **Months 1–2:** No revenue; sales and setup.
- **Month 3:** Half-month equivalent from first site ($1,400).
- **Month 4–5:** One site full month ($2,800/month).
- **Month 6:** 1.5 sites ($4,200).
- **Months 7–8:** Two sites ($5,600).
- **Month 9:** 2.5 sites ($7,000).
- **Months 10–12:** Three sites ($8,400).

No site is assumed to drop in this base case; in practice, one site could churn and be replaced, so "sites" is average count.

## Net Cash Flow (Commentary)

- **P&L above** is accrual (revenue when earned). **Cash flow** lags by AR timing: assume 30–45 days. So Month 4 revenue may be collected in Month 5–6. Cumulative net cash can be negative for more months than cumulative P&L; working capital or LOC covers the gap.
- **Conservative check:** If AR is 45 days, cash receipts in Year 1 are roughly 10–11 months of revenue equivalent. Revenue in table = $54,400; cash in might be ~$50,000; cash out = COGS + overhead ≈ $49,200 + $8,400 = $57,600. So cash out can exceed cash in in Year 1 without LOC or opening cash. Hence working capital / LOC need (Section 13).
