# Service Offering and Positioning

## Blvckshell — Facilities Execution and Accountability Operator

---

## Core Identity

Blvckshell is a **facilities execution and accountability operator** for multi-residential and light commercial properties in Ontario. It is not a cleaning company alone; it operates in the **friction layer between cleaning vendors and licensed trades**, providing recurring cleaning, unit turnovers, turnover audits, light maintenance, and facilities support under scope-locked, margin-governed contracts.

Differentiation is built on: **accountability**, **documentation**, **scope discipline**, **vendor consolidation**, and **portfolio readiness**. The company does not position as a full-service contractor, general contractor, or handyman service. It stays controlled and compliant.

---

## Service Architecture (Five Tiers)

### Tier 1: Recurring Cleaning

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Scope | Condo and commercial common-area cleaning: lobbies, hallways, elevators, washrooms, amenity spaces, garbage rooms, stairwells. |
| Delivery | Scheduled frequency (e.g. 2x, 3x, 4x per week); checklists and evidence-based reporting per visit. |
| Pricing | Flat monthly or scope-based; margin gate 25% (see Section 09). |
| Evidence | Photo and checklist completion; stored in internal audit/workflow system. |

---

### Tier 2: Unit Turnovers

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Scope | Full turnover cleaning; inspection-ready preparation; appliance wipe-down; interior finish reset. |
| Pricing | Per unit; tiered by unit size or condition; labour-estimated internally; margin ≥ 25%. |
| Delivery | Scheduled per vacancy; scope document per unit. |

---

### Tier 3: Turnover Readiness Audits

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Scope | Visual inspection only; photo documentation; readiness checklist; trade-required flags (no repair work). |
| Positioning | Low labour, high trust; supports handoff to cleaning or trades. |
| Pricing | Per unit or per audit; margin ≥ 30%. |
| Conversion | Pipeline to Tier 1 (cleaning), Tier 2 (turnover), or Tier 4 (maintenance). |

---

### Tier 4: Light Maintenance

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Scope | Door hardware replacement; bulb replacement (like-for-like); caulking; minor drywall patching; paint touch-ups; filter checks; cabinet hardware; cosmetic fixes. **Strictly non-licensed, non-permit work.** No structural modifications; no work requiring trade licenses or building permits. |
| Pricing | $55–75/hr band or flat-rate for common tasks; margin ≥ 30%. |
| Risk | Explicit exclusions and scope documentation; see Section 08 (Maintenance & Facilities Risk Controls). |

---

### Tier 5: Facilities Support and Coordination

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Scope | Vendor oversight; inspection reporting; post-trade validation; condition documentation; site presence coordination. |
| Pricing | Retainer or per-event; margin target ≥ 25%. |
| Role | Consolidates accountability and documentation for property managers; does not replace licensed trades. |

---

## Margin Requirements by Tier

| Tier | Minimum gross margin | Notes |
|------|----------------------|--------|
| Recurring cleaning | 25% (floor 20%) | Core recurring revenue. |
| Unit turnovers | ≥ 25% | Per-unit pricing; labour-estimated. |
| Turnover audits | ≥ 30% | Low labour; high margin. |
| Light maintenance | ≥ 30% | Higher margin to offset risk and training. |
| Facilities support | ≥ 25% | Retainer or per-event. |
| Pilots | 30–40% | Fixed-scope; prepaid/deposit; see Pilot Program Strategy (Section 07). |

## Definitive Scope (Will Do / Won't Do)

| Will do | Won't do |
|---------|----------|
| Common area and elevator cleaning; unit turnovers; turnover readiness audits; light maintenance (bulb replacement, door hardware adjustment, caulking, touch-ups, filter checks); facilities support and coordination. | Licensed trade work; major plumbing, electrical, or HVAC repairs; any permit-required or structural work. |

This summary tightens Tier 4 and overall risk wording; full exclusions are in Section 08.

---

## Out-of-Scope (Explicit Exclusions)

- **Licensed trades:** Electrical, plumbing, HVAC, gas, or any work requiring provincial trade qualification or permit.
- **Permit-required work:** Any work requiring building or other permits.
- **Structural modifications:** Load-bearing, framing, or structural changes.
- **Unit interior cleaning as open-ended recurring** (unless defined as Tier 1 or Tier 2 under contract).
- **Construction or post-construction cleanup** unless a separate, margin-compliant contract.
- **Capital equipment or certifications** not already planned (e.g. specialized floor care beyond basic equipment).

## Contract Structure

- **Term:** Typically 12 months for recurring; pilots and one-time work under fixed-scope agreements.
- **Pricing:** Fixed monthly, per unit, per hour, or per event as applicable; adjustments only by written amendment. HST stated clearly.
- **Scope:** Attached schedule or addendum; changes trigger change order or amendment. No scope creep without price adjustment.
- **Pilots:** Prepaid or deposit; no net-30 for pilot work; clearly bounded scope and conversion path (see Section 07).

---

## Quality and Evidence

- **Checklists:** Per tier and per site; completed with sign-off.
- **Evidence:** Photo and documentation where required; stored in internal system; supports QA and dispute resolution.
- **Reclean policy:** Defined for cleaning tier; add-ons at 30%+ margin where applicable.

## Summary

Blvckshell offers five integrated tiers: recurring cleaning, unit turnovers, turnover readiness audits, light maintenance, and facilities support. Positioning is facilities execution and accountability operator; Ontario regional; margin-governed; scope-locked. Contract structure and evidence discipline support margin control, retention, and conversion from pilots to recurring contracts.
