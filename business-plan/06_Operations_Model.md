# Operations Model

## Blvckshell — Facilities Execution and Accountability Operator

---

## Subcontractor Model

Initial delivery capacity is subcontractor-heavy. Blvckshell contracts with vetted individuals or small teams who perform site visits according to scope and checklist. The company retains client relationship, contract, pricing, QA, and billing; subcontractors are paid per agreed rate (payout ceiling) so that gross margin is preserved.

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Selection | Subcontractors are vetted for reliability, compliance (WSIB, insurance as required), and willingness to follow scope and documentation. For maintenance (Tier 4), subcontractors must accept scope exclusions (no licensed or permit work) and provide proof of insurance. |
| Agreement | Written agreement covering scope, rate, payment terms, insurance/WSIB obligations, and confidentiality. |
| Payout ceiling | Maximum labour cost per site or per visit is set so that gross margin stays within 20–30% (target 25%) for cleaning; ≥ 30% for maintenance. Subcontractor rates are negotiated within that ceiling. |
| Transition to employees | As volume and geography stabilize, a mix of employees and subcontractors may be used; the margin framework applies regardless. |

## Team Capacity Math

| Parameter | Conservative assumption |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| Productive hours per worker per day | 5–6 (after travel, setup, wrap-up) |
| Working days per month | 20–22 |
| Productive hours per worker per month | 100–132 |
| Sites per worker (recurring) | Depends on visit frequency and size; e.g. 2–4 visits per site per month implies 8–20 sites per full-time-equivalent if each visit is 1–2 hours |

**Example (illustrative):** One FTE at 110 hours/month, 2-hour average visit per site, 3 visits per site per month → 110 / (2 × 3) ≈ 18 sites per FTE. Conservative planning uses fewer sites per FTE (e.g. 8–12) to allow for travel, recleans, and variation.

## Organization and Role Evolution

Roles and structure evolve with scale. The following is a fully structured org-chart evolution plan; no role is added ahead of revenue and capacity need.

| Scale stage | Sites | Cities | Roles / structure | Notes |
|-------------|-------|--------|--------------------|--------|
| **Year 1** | 0–4 | 1 | Founder (sales, ops, finance); subcontractors only | No dedicated staff; founder does coordination, QA, billing. |
| **Year 2 (early)** | 4–6 | 1 | Founder + optional part-time coordinator or lead subcontractor | First delegation of scheduling or site coordination. |
| **Year 2 (mid)** | 6–10 | 1–2 | Founder + 0.5–1 FTE operations/coordination | Second city possible; one person cannot cover two cities alone. |
| **Year 3** | 10–18 | 2–3 | Founder + operations (1 FTE); subcontractors; optional part-time admin | Dedicated ops role; QA and client management structured. |
| **Year 4–5** | 18–30 | 3–5 | Founder + ops manager + optional city/regional lead; admin support | Management layer; possible regional or city-level lead for scheduling and QA. |
| **Scale (15+ cities)** | 75+ | 15+ | Central ops; regional/city leads; finance/reporting; field teams | Multi-layer; finance and reporting bank- and investor-ready. See Section 14. |

**Principles:** Contract-first and capacity-led; hiring follows signed work. Maintenance and multi-service require scope-trained subcontractors and clear oversight; the same structure supports cleaning and light maintenance with appropriate QA. Full org chart detail is in Appendix I.

## Route Density Strategy

- **Same city first.** All Year 1 sites are in Windsor (or one city) to minimize travel and simplify scheduling.
- **Clustering.** Where possible, sites are grouped so that one team can cover multiple buildings in a day or week, reducing deadhead travel.
- **No speculative routing.** Routes are built from signed contracts, not from assumed future wins.

## QA Enforcement Structure

| Layer | Mechanism |
|-------|------------|
| Scope | Contract and site schedule define areas, frequency, and standards. |
| Checklist | Per-visit checklist (site-specific or template); completion required before sign-off. |
| Evidence | Photo or other evidence as required by contract or policy; stored in internal system. |
| Review | Supervisor or manager spot-checks and reviews completions; recleans triggered when standard not met. |
| Client | Formal or informal walk-throughs; feedback loop for corrections. |

The internal audit and workflow system supports assignment, completion capture, and approval. Failures (e.g. missed item, client complaint) trigger reclean and, if repeated, corrective action.

---

## Reclean Control Policy

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| When | Reclean is required when visit does not meet checklist or client standard. |
| Who decides | Blvckshell or client per contract; typically Blvckshell identifies and schedules. |
| Cost | Recleans may be included in base (limited number per period) or billed as add-on at 30%+ margin. Policy is clear in contract or policy doc. |
| Cap | Excessive recleans indicate scope, training, or fit issues; addressed operationally and, if needed, contractually. |

Recleans are tracked so that margin and root cause are visible.

## Incident Handling Process

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1. Report | Worker or subcontractor reports incident (injury, damage, security, other) to Blvckshell. |
| 2. Document | Incident form: date, location, description, people involved, witnesses if any. |
| 3. Notify | Client notified per contract; insurer notified if required. |
| 4. Follow-up | Investigation and resolution per policy; WSIB or insurance process if applicable. |
| 5. Record | Incident logged for compliance and pattern analysis; no concealment. |

---

## Billing Enforcement Policy

| Element | Policy |
|---------|--------|
| Invoicing | Invoices sent per contract terms (e.g. monthly in arrears); clear due date. |
| Payment terms | Target 30 days; 45 days accepted only where contract or client requires it. |
| Follow-up | Overdue accounts receive structured follow-up (reminder, then escalation). |
| Scope changes | Changes in scope or frequency require written change order or amendment; no informal scope creep without price adjustment. |
| Disputes | Disputed amounts are resolved per contract; undisputed amount paid while dispute is resolved where possible. |

AR timing is built into cash flow and working capital (Section 13).

## Documentation Discipline

- **Contract:** Signed agreement with scope, price, term, termination.
- **Site schedule:** Areas, frequency, checklist reference.
- **Completions:** Checklist and evidence per visit; retained for QA and dispute resolution.
- **Incidents:** Logged and retained per incident process.
- **Financial:** P&L, cash flow, site-level economics; suitable for bank and internal use.

Operational flow (described in text):

1. **Contract signed** → Site added to system; scope and schedule defined.
2. **Assignment** → Visits assigned to worker/subcontractor; checklist and instructions provided.
3. **Visit** → Worker performs work; completes checklist; submits evidence if required.
4. **Review** → Supervisor or system flags incomplete or fail; reclean scheduled if needed.
5. **Approval** → Visit approved; completion closed.
6. **Billing** → Invoice generated per contract; sent to client; payment tracked.
7. **Payment** → Cash received; AR updated; cash flow reflected.

This flow is supported by the internal audit and workflow system; the exact system design is internal and not sold to clients.

## Operational KPI Dashboard (Definition and Review)

The following operational KPIs are defined, tracked, and reviewed (at least monthly) to support margin control and execution:

| KPI | Definition | Target / use |
|-----|------------|-------------|
| Revenue per site | Monthly contract value per site (blended or per site) | Compare to $2,800 benchmark; flag sites below target margin. |
| Gross margin per site | (Revenue − direct cost) / Revenue per site | Target 20–30%; alert if below 20%. |
| Reclean rate | Recleans as % of completed visits (or per site per period) | Track trend; investigate if rising; cap excessive recleans. |
| AR days | Average days from invoice to payment | Target 30–45; escalate overdue. |
| Payout ratio | Labour (payout) / Revenue per site or in aggregate | Must stay within payout ceiling; alert if exceeding. |
| Renewal rate | Sites renewed / sites up for renewal in period | Target 80%+; analyze causes of non-renewal. |
| Sites (count) | Active contracted sites | Versus plan; trigger for capacity and expansion decisions. |

### Maintenance KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Target / use |
|-----|------------|-------------|
| Maintenance revenue % | Maintenance revenue / total revenue | Track mix; target ramp as capacity allows; see Section 09 blended margin. |
| Maintenance gross margin % | (Maintenance revenue − direct cost) / Maintenance revenue | Target ≥ 30%; alert if below. |
| Maintenance rework rate | Rework or callback jobs / total maintenance jobs | Keep low; root cause if rising. |
| Avg maintenance job value | Total maintenance revenue / number of jobs | Monitor for scope creep or under-pricing. |

### Pilot KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Target / use |
|-----|------------|-------------|
| Pilot conversion rate | Pilots converted to recurring (or repeat) / total pilots in period | Conservative 30–50%; track by pilot type. |
| Pilot margin | Gross margin on pilot revenue | Target 30–40%; conservative 30%. |
| Pilot AR | Receivables from pilot work | Should be zero or minimal (prepaid/deposit). |
| Pilot → recurring pipeline ratio | Value of converted pilots / total pilot revenue (trailing) | Measures pipeline effectiveness; no inflation of Year 1 revenue. |

These metrics are reviewed monthly (or at contract/renewal events) so that underperformance is caught early and corrective action is taken. Stating this in the plan increases perceived operational sophistication and lender confidence.

## Summary

Operations are built on a subcontractor-heavy model with payout ceilings, capacity math, and route density. Multi-service delivery (cleaning, turnover, maintenance, facilities support) uses the same discipline: scope-locked, margin-governed, evidence-based. QA, reclean, incident, and billing policies are defined and enforced. Maintenance and pilot KPIs are tracked alongside core cleaning metrics. Documentation supports margin control, compliance, and client retention.
