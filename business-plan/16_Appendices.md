## A. Key Financial Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Revenue** | Contract value earned in the period (accrual). Typically monthly contract value × months in period for recurring sites; add-ons when earned. |
| **COGS (Cost of goods sold)** | Direct cost of delivering service: labour (subcontractor payout or employee wages and burden), supplies, and other direct site costs. |
| **Gross profit** | Revenue minus COGS. |
| **Gross margin** | Gross profit / Revenue (expressed as percentage). |
| **Overhead** | Fixed operating expenses not allocated to a single site: insurance, professional fees, software, marketing, admin, office (if any). |
| **EBITDA** | Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, and amortization. For this business: gross profit minus overhead and (when applicable) founder/employee salaries. Depreciation and amortization are minimal in Year 1–2. |
| **Net P&L** | Profit or loss after all revenue and expenses in the period. |
| **Working capital** | Short-term assets (e.g. receivables) minus short-term liabilities (e.g. payables). Positive working capital implies cash tied up in AR until collected. |
| **LOC** | Line of credit; revolving facility for working capital use. |

## B. Margin Formulas

| Formula | Use |
|---------|-----|
| Gross margin = (Revenue − COGS) / Revenue | Check site or company margin. |
| Target gross margin = 25%. So COGS = Revenue × (1 − 0.25) = 0.75 × Revenue (at target). | Pricing and cost control. |
| Payout ceiling (max labour) = Revenue − (Revenue × Target margin) − Supplies − Other direct = Revenue × (1 − Target margin) − Supplies − Other direct. | Set maximum labour cost per site so margin is met. |
| Break-even revenue = Fixed costs / Gross margin %. E.g. $8,400 / 0.25 = $33,600. | Break-even analysis. |

## C. Capacity Formulas

| Formula | Use |
|---------|-----|
| Sites per FTE = Productive hours per FTE per month / Hours per site per month. | How many sites one FTE can serve. |
| Hours per site per month = Visits per site per month × Average hours per visit. | From contract (frequency) and typical visit length. |
| Productive hours per FTE per month = Working days × Hours per day (after travel, etc.). E.g. 22 days × 5 hrs = 110. | Capacity planning. |
| Teams required = Total sites / Sites per FTE (rounded up). | Staffing and subcontractor count. |

## D. Regulatory Summary

| Area | Requirement (Ontario) |
|------|------------------------|
| **WSIB** | Workplace Safety and Insurance Board coverage for applicable workers. Registration and premium payment required. Subcontractors must have their own coverage or be covered by principal. |
| **HST** | Goods and Services Tax / Harmonized Sales Tax. Cleaning services are generally taxable. Registrants collect HST and remit to CRA. Contract should state whether prices are tax-inclusive or plus HST. |
| **Insurance** | Commercial general liability (CGL) and other coverages as required by clients (e.g. $2M–$5M). Certificates of insurance provided to clients; renewals tracked. |
| **Contract law** | Contracts governed by Ontario law. Written agreements recommended; scope, price, term, and termination should be clear. |
| **Employment / subcontractor** | Correct classification of workers (employee vs. subcontractor) per CRA and employment standards. Subcontractor agreements should be in writing and specify insurance, WSIB, and confidentiality. |

This is a summary only; the company is responsible for full compliance. Professional advice should be sought where appropriate.

## J. Lender-Ready Risk Disclosure Addendum

This addendum is intended for use in bank or lender discussions. It does not replace Section 08 (Risk Management and Compliance) but provides a consolidated, lender-facing summary of material risks and mitigations. Lenders should conduct their own due diligence.

### Purpose

To disclose material risks associated with Blvckshell's operations and financing request, and to summarize mitigation. Risk cannot be eliminated; this document does not constitute a guarantee of repayment or performance.

### Material Risks (Summary)

| Risk category | Description | Mitigation summary |
|---------------|-------------|---------------------|
| **Revenue concentration** | Early-stage reliance on a small number of sites; loss of one or two contracts has material revenue impact. | Strict contract terms, scope clarity, retention focus; pilot pipeline for diversification; LOC and working capital for timing. |
| **Margin compression** | Labour or supply cost increase, or client price pressure, could reduce gross margin below target. | Margin governance (target 25%, floor 20%); payout ceiling; add-on and maintenance minimum 30%; reprice or exit at renewal if margin cannot be restored. |
| **Slow ramp** | Revenue may grow more slowly than projected; break-even delayed. | Conservative forecast; no speculative hiring or geography; zero founder salary in Year 1; LOC for working capital. |
| **Liquidity** | AR timing (30–45 days) and seasonal variation may create cash shortfalls. | Working capital planning; LOC request (Section 13); no net-30 on pilot work (prepaid/deposit). |
| **Regulatory** | WSIB, HST, insurance non-compliance would create liability and contract risk. | WSIB and insurance in place before first work; HST registered; subcontractor compliance verified; no work outside scope (e.g. no licensed or permit work). |
| **Maintenance and facilities** | Light maintenance could expose company to claims if scope is exceeded or work is misclassified. | Explicit exclusions (no licensed, permit, or structural work); scope documentation; evidence logging; insurance and subcontractor requirements (Section 08). |
| **Key person** | Founder carries sales, operations, and financial oversight in Year 1–2. | Documentation and systems; plan does not assume irreplaceability in long term. |

### Acknowledgment

- The company has disclosed known material risks and mitigations to the best of its current knowledge.
- Projections and forecasts are conservative and subject to change; actual results will vary.
- The lender is encouraged to conduct independent due diligence and to obtain legal, accounting, or other professional advice as appropriate.
- This addendum is part of the business plan and is not a separate legal or contractual document unless otherwise agreed in writing.
