## Business Model

Blvckshell is a facilities execution and accountability operator for multi-residential and light commercial properties. It provides recurring cleaning, unit turnovers, turnover readiness audits, light maintenance (non-licensed), and facilities support under contract. Clients are property management companies, condominium corporations, and building owners. Service is delivered under contract (monthly, per-unit, or per-event) with defined scope and margin governance. Pilot programs offer low-friction entry with conversion to recurring contracts.

The company does not sell technology or software. An internal audit and workflow system is used to manage assignments, evidence, approvals, and documentation. This system supports margin control and quality enforcement; it is not a separate product or revenue line.

---

## Service Focus

- **Tier 1:** Recurring cleaning of common areas (lobbies, hallways, elevators, washrooms, garbage rooms, stairwells).
- **Tier 2:** Unit turnovers (full turnover cleaning, inspection-ready).
- **Tier 3:** Turnover readiness audits (inspection only; photo documentation).
- **Tier 4:** Light maintenance (non-licensed, non-permit: hardware, bulbs, caulking, touch-ups, filters).
- **Tier 5:** Facilities support and coordination (vendor oversight, reporting).
- **Out of scope:** Licensed trades, permit-required work, structural work; unit interiors as open-ended recurring unless under contract; construction cleanup unless separate margin-compliant contract.

## Operating Philosophy

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| Conservative | No speculative hiring or geography; growth follows signed contracts. |
| Margin-disciplined | Target 25% gross margin; floor 15% under stress; add-ons above 30%. |
| Subcontractor-heavy initially | Labour capacity is built via vetted subcontractors to limit fixed payroll in Year 1. |
| No founder salary in Year 1 | Founder compensation is deferred to preserve cash and align with break-even focus. |
| Low fixed overhead | Office, admin, and tooling kept minimal until revenue supports them. |
| No speculative hiring | Roles are added when workload and revenue justify them, not on forecast alone. |
